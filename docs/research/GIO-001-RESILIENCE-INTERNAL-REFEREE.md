### Exact pencil calculation

For one deleted flag (a,b), left projections have q=a, l=b, p any of s other points of b, r any of s lines through p other than b. Middle projections have p=a, l=b, q any of s other points of b, r any of s other lines through a. Right projections have p=a,r=b, l any of s lines through a other than b, q any of s other points of l. The generalized quadrangle axiom prevents any 3-cycle, so q is never incident r in these configurations. Each (q,r) has a unique projection q−l−p−r, so within each position there are s² distinct pairs; across the three positions, the deleted flag would have to occupy two different flags of the same simple length-three path, which is impossible. Overlaps among distinct deleted flags only decrease the union. Under separately injected labels, each q,r yields four distinct ordered physical endpoint pairs. No hidden replacement path is permitted through deleted anchors.

### Independence and probability scrutiny

A signed GF5 support event for one sixset depends on exactly its own six original coefficient variables. The claimed lower probability is for ONE fixed balanced sign pattern, not the sum over ten sign patterns, not the whole R3 probability, and not one chosen by observing sampled coefficients. Preselect the actual flag-disjoint sixsets using f/g and the deletion argument; then preselect a sign per sixset using only its original dual template. Under independently sampled columns the indicators are genuinely independent even if their **physical coordinate symbols overlap**. If a later adversary deletes source flags to hit every realized signed event, X disjoint successful events necessitate at least X deletions. This does not establish an efficient extraction or high-probability guarantee under dependent coefficients.
