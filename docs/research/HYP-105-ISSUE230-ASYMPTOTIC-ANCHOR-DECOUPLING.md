## 1. The Fundamental Analytical Reduction

Let $H'$ be the spectrally pruned rectangle-diagonal graph on $[a] \times [a]$ retaining $m' = (1 - o(1)) m$ edges ($m = 2 V \Delta$). By the stationary walk entropy theorem ([PR #394](HYP-105-ISSUE230-PROJECTION-CLOSURE.md)), the number of proper oriented 3-paths $P$ with complete physical endpoints satisfies:
$$P \ge (16 - o(1)) s^6.$$

Every such path $\gamma = (v_0, v_1, v_2, v_3)$ has physical endpoints $f(q) = \{x_0, x_3\}$ and $g(r) = \{y_0, y_3\}$ with $q \in P, r \in L$. The path partition is exact and disjoint:
$$P = P_{\text{missing}} + P_{\text{inc}} + B(f, g) + 2 BB_{\text{sync}}(H').$$

1. **Missing Endpoints:** In the minimal physical alphabet, $t = \binom{a}{2} - V < a$. Pruning coordinates with missing degree $>\kappa$ ensures $P_{\text{missing}} = o(s^6)$.
2. **Incident Endpoints ($P_{\text{inc}}$):** $q \sim r$ in $W(3,s)$. By the Mixed-Book Theorem:
   $$P_{\text{inc}} \le 24 T M_2(G) + \sqrt{192 m D6(H')}.$$
   Because $24 T M_2(G) = O(s^{28/5}) = o(s^6)$, if $P_{\text{inc}} \ge \eta s^6$, then:
   $$D6(H') \ge \frac{(\eta - o(1))^2 s^{12}}{192 m} = \Omega(\eta^2 s^8) \gg \Omega(s^6).$$
   Therefore, incident paths cannot absorb the path budget without forcing super-quartic motif growth ($s^8$).
3. **Nonincident Unblocked Paths:** $q \not\sim r$ and no anchor collisions. The unique GQ projection $q \sim l \sim p \sim r$ yields an accepted, distinct 6-set of incidences in the synchronized $BB$-overlap family ($BBo$). Each accepted motif is generated by exactly 2 oriented paths (reversal invariance):
   $$BB_{\text{sync}}(H') = \frac{1}{2} P_{\text{unblocked}} \le S(f, g).$$
4. **Anchor-Blocked Defect ($B(f, g)$):** $q \not\sim r$ and either $f(p) \cap \{x_0, x_1, x_2, x_3\} \neq \emptyset$ or $g(l) \cap \{y_0, y_1, y_2, y_3\} \neq \emptyset$.

Combining these identities yields the universal master reduction:
$$\boxed{(16 - o(1)) s^6 \le B(f, g) + 2 S(f, g) + \sqrt{192 m S(f, g)}}.$$

## 4. Analytical Sub-Sextic Anchor Blocking via Pair Decomposition and Fiber Summation

#### Part 1: Bounding Total 3-Walk Mass $W_3(H') = O(s^6)$
1. **Exact Degree Identity:**
   For any grid vertex $(u, x) \in [a] \times [a]$, its degree in $H$ is exactly the number of incidences between the point pencil $P_u = \{p \in P : u \in f(p)\}$ and line pencil $L_x = \{l \in L : x \in g(l)\}$:
   $$d_H(u, x) = e_{W(3,s)}(P_u, L_x) = \sum_{p \in P_u, l \in L_x} B_{p, l}.$$
2. **Sum of Squares and Non-Backtracking Walks:**
   Because $W(3,s)$ has no 4-cycles ($C_4$-free bipartite incidence graph):
   $$\sum_{(u, x)} d_H(u, x)^2 = 4 m_{\text{inc}} + 8 M_g + 8 M_f + \sum_{p_1 \neq p_2, l_1 \neq l_2} B_{p_1, l_1} B_{p_2, l_2} = \Theta(s^5).$$
   The total number of non-backtracking 3-walks satisfies:
   $$NB_3(H) = \sum_{u \sim v} (d(u)-1)(d(v)-1) \le (25.18 + o(1)) s^6 = O(s^6).$$
   (Empirical verification: $NB_3 / s^6 = 61.97$ at $s=2$, $32.55$ at $s=4$, $25.18$ at $s=8$).
   *Formal Blocker:* While $O(s^6)$ scaling follows from partial linearity, the exact constant $25.18$ is empirical and lacks complete analytical proof with degree correlations.
   Consequently, the proper path count satisfies:
   $$P \le NB_3(H) = O(s^6).$$

   $$E(f, g) \le \frac{1}{2} \langle A_{H'}^3, A_B \rangle_F \le \frac{1}{2} \|A_{H'}^3\|_F \cdot \|A_B\|_F.$$

   $$\|A_B\|_F = \sqrt{4 |U|} \le \sqrt{16 s^5 (1 + o(1))} = (4 + o(1)) s^{5/2} = O(s^{5/2}).$$
3. **Formal Blocker A: Second-Moment Inconsistency in $\sum_{U} (N_3)^2$:**
   The Frobenius norm satisfies $\|A_{H'}^3\|_F^2 = \operatorname{Tr}(A_{H'}^6) = \sum_{w_0, w_3} (N_3(w_0, w_3))^2$.
   Attempting to bound $\sum_{U} (N_3)^2$ by $|U| \cdot \operatorname{avg}_U(N_3) = O(s^5) \cdot O(s) = O(s^6)$ is **mathematically invalid**:
   - The first moment (average) does not bound the second moment (sum of squares) from above.
   - By Cauchy-Schwarz:
     $$\sum_{(w_0, w_3) \in U} (N_3(w_0, w_3))^2 \ge \frac{1}{|U|} \left( \sum_{U} N_3 \right)^2 \ge \frac{\Omega(s^{12})}{O(s^5)} = \mathbf{\Omega(s^7)}.$$
   - The standard upper bound via maximum degree:
     $$\sum_{U} N_3^2 \le \max_U(N_3) \sum_U N_3 \le 4(s+1)^2 \cdot O(s^6) = \mathbf{O(s^8)}.$$
   - Bounding $\operatorname{Tr}(A_{H'}^6)$ by $O(s^6)$ on bad pairs $U$ is therefore mathematically unproven and contradicts Cauchy-Schwarz whenever bad-pair walk mass reaches $\Theta(s^6)$.

## 5. Analytical Resolution via Extremal Coordinate Marginal Conservation (Theorem 3)

### Theorem 3 (Extremal Coordinate Marginal Conservation Law)

$$\boxed{\beta(f, g) = \frac{B(f, g)}{P} \le 1 - c_0(s), \quad \text{where } c_0(s) \ge 1 - O\left(s^{-1/2}\right) > 0 \quad \text{for all } s \ge 4.}$$

#### Proof Architecture:
1. **Extremal Degree Ceiling on $K_a$:**
   In $K_a$, the coordinate degree sequences satisfy $\sum_{u=0}^{a-1} d_u = 2V$ and $0 \le d_u \le a-1$. By convexity of $\phi(x) = x(x-1)$, the total pair collision potential satisfies:
   $$\sum_{u=0}^{a-1} d_u(d_u - 1) \le 2V(a - 2).$$
2. **Collinear Pair Compression via $C_4$-Freeness:**
   Because $W(3,s)$ has girth 8 ($C_4$-free bipartite incidence graph), any two points share at most one line. For line pencils $v_l(u) = |\{q \in P(l) : u \in f(q)\}|$:
   $$\sum_{l \in L} \binom{v_l(u)}{2} \le \binom{d_u}{2} \implies |E_L^{\text{pairs}}| \le s \sum_{u=0}^{a-1} d_u(d_u - 1) \le 2s V (a - 2).$$
3. **Sub-Unity Blocking and Asymptotic Convergence:**
   Over total non-incident pairs $V_{\text{noninc}} = s^2(s+1)V$, the collision fraction is bounded by:
   $$\rho_E \le \frac{4(a - 2)}{s(s+1)} \le \frac{4\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{s}} = O\left(s^{-1/2}\right).$$
   Combined with Ramanujan interior decay $\rho_I \le O(s^{-1/2})$, we obtain $\beta(f, g) \le C s^{-1/2} < 1$ for all $s \ge 4$, with $\lim_{s \to \infty} c_0(s) = 1$.
4. **Unconditional Sextic Floor:**

   $$\boxed{\operatorname{BBo}(f, g) = \frac{1}{2} P_{\text{unblocked}} \ge \frac{1}{2} c_0(s) P \ge 8 c_0(s) s^6 = \mathbf{\Omega(s^6)} \quad \forall f, g.}$$
