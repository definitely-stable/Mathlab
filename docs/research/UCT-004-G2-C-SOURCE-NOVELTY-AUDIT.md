## Closest original sources and why they matter

- **LIT-149:** Chandrima Kayal, Sophie Laplante, Émile Larroque, Krišjānis Prūsis and Jevgēnijs Vihrovs, [*Certification complexity of Boolean functions*, arXiv:2609.26757](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26757), 22 September 2026, [ECCC TR26-206](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/206/). The authors develop an **operational notion of certification** and characterize known classical randomized/quantum certificate measures through other query-complexity notions, including sabotage and unambiguous certificates. **Very high novelty-overlap risk**: our projection-separable witness-cover equivalence is not to be announced as a newly discovered general "certification complexity" formalism without line-by-line comparison to their exact definitions and proofs. The original paper has NOT been independently proved by Mathlab.
- **LIT-142:** Scott Aaronson, [*Quantum Certificate Complexity*, JCSS 2008](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcss.2007.06.020): formal randomized certificate complexity, not a clean-sheet invention here.
- **LIT-143:** Ambainis–Kokainis–Prūsis–Vihrovs–Zajakins, [*All Classical Adversary Methods Are Equivalent for Total Functions*, ACM TOCT 2021](https://doi.org/10.1145/3442357): fractional block sensitivity and classical randomized adversary baselines. A finite cover is a **certificate/data query game**, not yet a novel lower-bound technique.
- **LIT-144:** Ben-David & Kothari, [*Randomized Query Complexity Can Beat Certificate Complexity*, 2026-09](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15063): source-claimed bounded-error randomized query complexity can be much lower than deterministic certificate complexity; G2-C perfect-completeness and fixed-witness soundness are **different** than solving the original function by a bounded-error decision tree without prover.
- **LIT-150:** Ben-Sasson–Harsha–Lachish–Matsliah, [*Sound 3-Query PCPPs Are Long*, ACM TOCT 2009](https://doi.org/10.1145/1595391.1595394): relates proof-length to **proximity** soundness in PCPP models. Opposite parity can differ by only one bit (normalized Hamming distance 1/n); standard constant-distance proximity soundness cannot be substituted for G2-C's rejection of **every** opposite parity state.
- **LIT-114**, Pătraşcu–Tarniţă 2007 dynamic partial sums in the bit-probe model: the original theorem family already couples reads and updates, but **does not automatically incorporate** this G2-C free-program, malicious fixed-witness certificate game.
- **LIT-129/131**, annotated streams and online Merlin–Arthur: require timed witness publication, persistent verifier memory, protocol communication, adversarial interaction and soundness, all absent from the G2-C *local bit-probe-only* cost ledger.

## What is actually established vs open

**PROVED_DERIVED_CLASSICAL:** For fixed-length b-bit witnesses chosen independently of private coins and a verifier reading at most p data bits, alpha=0 and **some** delta<1 are equivalent to a cover of the parity class by at most 2^b p-projection-separable sets. The construction uses public hard-coded tables indexed by (n,a,π,y), may have exponential program description, selects uniformly random *opposite-parity* y and gives soundness delta≤1−2^(1−n). Thus this does NOT prove a useful constant error or efficient algorithm.

**FINITE_EXACT:** κ(n,p) computed for n≤5 with separate exhaustive subset/projection search and exact set-cover certificates; equality with 2^(ceil(n/p)−1) for these tiny n only. The explicit six-state (n4,p3) and twelve-state (n5,p4) one-witness nonlinear fibers **refute the affine rank fiber cardinality bound outside its linear scope**, but do not refute the original nonlinear b-lower-bound conjecture.

**CONJECTURE:** Whether κ(n,p)=2^(ceil(n/p)−1) holds for **all** n and 1≤p≤n. Do not claim this from tests. In particular, the existence of an exact finite characterization by an exponential brute-force search is NOT a construction of an efficient dynamic Rust primitive or a new lower-bound method.

**Higher-value G3 frontier:** explicit fully costed online witness maintenance, program description length, honest prover complexity, persistent authenticated state, network bytes, multiple/adaptive queries, meaningful δ≤1/3 and randomized key/security contracts. Check exact original 2026 source theorem overlap first; pursue only if a measurable mathematical separation remains.

**CI gate:** `research/test_uct004_nonlinear_cover.py` independently tests all nonempty honest-state subsets up to n=5, projection separability for all opposite states, inclusion-maximal candidate covers, exact set-cover minima, both parities and the n4 nonlinear falsifier. This is finite computational evidence, not peer-reviewed theorem novelty or a general-n proof.
