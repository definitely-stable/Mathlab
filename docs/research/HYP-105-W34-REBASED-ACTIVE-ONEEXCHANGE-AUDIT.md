### Formal termination lemma (not full-zero convergence)

For a finite complete GF5 source assignment with
nonnegative integer R3, if the algorithm accepts
ONLY strict-R3-decreasing one-source changes
while preserving R2 nonincrease, then each accepted
move reduces R3 by at least one. Therefore a
particular monotone sequence beginning at current
R3=r accepts at most r changes, cannot cycle,
and eventually terminates in an R2-guarded
one-exchange local minimum OR R3=0.
This is an elementary finite termination theorem,
**NOT** a guarantee that the final R3 equals zero.
The new source can have a completely different
active hypergraph and candidate ordering.

58 on lex and 65 on reverse-line. Thus any
monotone single-flag-only descent following this

accepted changes respectively; it may stop
much earlier at a nonzero local minimum.
This bound does not quantify over a
nonmonotone or multi-source strategy.
