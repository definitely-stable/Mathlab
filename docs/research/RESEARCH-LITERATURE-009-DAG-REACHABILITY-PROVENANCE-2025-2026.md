## Mathematical boundaries and priority

1. **Critical strict DAG lower bound: `LIT-298`.** Larsen–Yu explicitly prove `tilde-Omega(log^(3/2) n)` in the **dynamic cell-probe** reachability model under edge insertions. Citation lineage FOCS 2023, SIAM first online 13 February 2025, 2026 bound volume: record bibliographic year **2025** to avoid falsely new theorem. The source does *not* automatically cover a DAG restricted to new-sink appends, local/immutable per-node labels, state-dependent paid manifest, arbitrary read/write blocks or exact trust roots; require a formal model-preserving reduction before invoking it in DAG-002 or UCT-005.
2. **Cut-query oracle is separate (`LIT-302`).** Knowing the existence of edges crossing a queried cut is not a `c`-bit cell probe. Near-linear cut-query reachability/topological sorting does not solve physical index queries at those probe costs.
3. **Static reachability / dynamic transitive closure (`LIT-299,300,301,307`).** Chain widths, partial two-hop labels, transitive-closure representations and offline contraction are distinct from online old-label-immutable publication (DAG-002); judge full memory, update, query and physical page recourse simultaneously.
4. **Topological order (`LIT-305,306`).** Edge-insertion order with order maintenance/cycle detection changes old nodes; evidence of known algorithms, not a DAG-002 immutable-label counterexample.
5. **Compression (`LIT-303,304`).** Dual clique cover and preferential attachment graph entropy use undirected/specialized graph classes; their static compressed representation and graph primitives do not imply online compressed update locality or crash safe WAL/GC.
6. **Temporal evidence and GraphRAG (`LIT-308..316`).** Distinguish temporal valid-time from transaction epoch; entity memory forecast, explicit source citations, temporal window connected components and graph-derived generated answers have different oracle contracts. No cited experiment establishes cryptographic freshness/completeness or universal graph superiority vs BM25/dense.
