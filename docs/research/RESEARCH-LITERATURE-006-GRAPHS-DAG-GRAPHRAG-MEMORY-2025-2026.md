# IMPORT-006 — Graphs, immutable DAG, GraphRAG, graph-based agent memory (source/model audit)

Status: **SOURCE METADATA AUDITED / NO INDEPENDENT THEOREM OR BENCHMARK REPLICATION**. Date: 2026-10-09. Tracking: [issue #184](https://github.com/definitely-stable/Mathlab/issues/184).

## Research model boundaries

- `graph-algorithms`: mathematical reachability, label/space/time tradeoffs and DAG updates; prior Mathlab `DAG-002`, `ALG-001`, `GRAPH-001` and existing LIT-160..198 are **retained**.
- `graph-reasoning`: graph or DAG of inference steps, with no guarantee that LLM-generated steps are valid.
- `graph-rag`: retrieval over document/entity graph; measure graph extraction cost, update cost, query latency, multi-hop answer faithfulness and source provenance against plain vector/BM25 RAG, not merely retrieval recall.
- `agent-memory`: temporal fact changes, causal and entity relations, memory CRUD, provenance, invalidation, deletion, conflicts and long-horizon answer stability. Episodic graphs must not silently collapse into static append-only DAGs.

**Important negative result:** 2026 ICLR GraphRAG-Bench explicitly studies tasks where GraphRAG underperforms plain RAG; no blanket "graphs improve RAG" claim. Compare same corpus, tokenizer, cost budget, model, baseline, timestamps, evaluation and contamination controls.

New corpus: **29** distinct identities, including 2026 conference and September 2026 preprint sources; 2024/2010 originals retained for historical graph baselines. Numbering starts after LIT-213 and does not rewrite historical IDs.

## Verification, dedup and next research gates

1. Identity: DOI publisher or arXiv author abstract is the primary bibliography; arXiv posting year can differ from peer-reviewed venue year. Avoid double counting LightRAG 2024 preprint and EMNLP 2025 publication, and ICLR 2026 papers with 2025 preprints.
2. Tier: `publisher_abstract_checked` for publisher DOI pages, `primary_abstract_checked` for arXiv papers. Neither means full proof, reproducible runtime or peer-reviewed status unless independently verified.
3. Graph/DAG: benchmark append-only immutable reachability and dynamic graph mutation separately; compare transitive-closure size, chain-width, update complexity and metadata bytes. Existing Mathlab LIT-187 Incremental Reachability Index MUST NOT be re-imported.
4. GraphRAG: paired fixed-corpus evaluation plain BM25+vector / GraphRAG / on-demand LogicRAG; include long-tail updates, removal and poisoning, exact resource ledger and answer provenance.
5. Memory: separate semantic, causal, temporal and entity edges; compare Hindsight, MAGMA, Zep, MRAgent and Mem0 with counterfactual revisions, deletions, evidence tracing and explicit as-of timestamp.
6. No inferred transitive novelty: graph theoretic reachability ≠ chain-of-thought reliability; evidence/citation link ≠ truth proof; LLM accuracy ≠ formal theorem.
