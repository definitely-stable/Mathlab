## 3. Why this matters and what it does NOT show

The chosen twelve source flags form two saturated \(K_{3,2}\) blocks in the same \(G\); all source point/line IDs are distinct, and the full geometry is unchanged. The contrast persists even with fixed labels of **ALL original source points**, so it is not explainable by merely changing the source geometry or selected density/degree/codegree profile. The only freedom is how **unselected genuine source lines** receive still-available physical pair labels.

It follows that the proof strategy "many saturated K3,2 in the retained graph => at least one fresh full-H two-ID extension per saturated block" is **false**, even for a fully legal finite source model and before asking for asymptotic coefficients. One cannot promote the unit-multiplicity certificate of #664 into a universal positive theorem without an additional lower bound using the completion geometry, or a same-map compensation from other accepted GF5 classes.

**Boundary:** a finite W34 example does **not** contradict a claim for all sufficiently large \(s\) if that claim assumes additional asymptotic mass/distribution conditions; such conditions remain to be proposed and proved. The zero map can have many accepted original sixsets completely unrelated to these two parents. Do not infer seven-class \(o(s^6)\), an ASET exponent, or a universal no-go to the entire research program.
