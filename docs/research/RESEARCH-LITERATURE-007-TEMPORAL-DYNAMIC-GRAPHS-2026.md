## Exact model separation

- **Point-vs-interval:** instant contact on a path, edge-availability interval, bitemporal transaction/valid-time snapshot and temporal journey with waiting are four different semantics. SAND 2026.5 is a critical source proving models cannot simply be interchanged.
- **Directed vs undirected, offline vs online:** FPT/XP path-shift feasibility, temporal cut, statically indexed reachability and fully dynamic membership entail distinct update/read claims.
- **Succinct graphs:** compressed graph bits and word-RAM operations do not imply minimum storage device page recourse under actual compaction, WAL, and snapshot retention.
- **Graph retrieval vs agent memory:** GraphFlow, GraphAnchor, IA-RAG, ActMem, DYNA and T-GRAG are empirical author models. Measure extraction latency, source mutation, stale answers, context costs, accuracy, and adversarial hallucination against lexical and vector baselines; never claim universal graph wins. LIT-221 and LIT-243 remain explicit negative controls.
- **Novelty check:** LIT-187 incremental reachability, LIT-189 temporal reachability recognition, LIT-196 dynamic directed sparsification, TKG-001 G0 and the ROST UCT-005 roots are already indexed. Do not import them again or transfer their theorem claims to graph-RAG.
