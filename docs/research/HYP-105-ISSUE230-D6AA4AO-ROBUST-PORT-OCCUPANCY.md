Fix a real original AA4-AL D pair e={(p,l),(q,m)}, p!=q and l!=m, under the ONE globally fixed actual f,g. On either typed physical side, the two pinned original source factors have physical labels {c,u} and {c,v}, with c,u,v three different physical coordinates and c the already completed degree-two vertex. Let the auxiliary coordinate set X= [a]\{c,u,v}, n=|X|=a-3. Let M be the set of globally missing physical unordered-pair labels in the chosen complete injective typed map, with

    m=|M|=C(a,2)-V, 0<=m<=a-2=n+1

by minimality of a. Thus the entire globally missing-edge set has LINEAR rather than quadratic cardinality in a. Define d_u=|{{u,x}∈M:x∈X}|, d_v similarly; let m_X=|{{x,y}∈M:x,y∈X}|. The port-to-port missing edge {u,v} and edges incident to c do not occur in the four-edge P5 residual path except indirectly through the total m budget.

A distinct ordered auxiliary triple (x,y,z) ∈ X³, all different, yields a formally valid P5 completion with physical edges

    u-x, x-y, y-z, z-v.

There are n(n-1)(n-2) such physical P5 edge multisets. A missing physical u-x destroys at most (n-1)(n-2) ordered triples; similarly a missing v-z. A missing INTERNAL unordered pair {r,t}⊂X destroys at most 4(n-2) triples: two orientations and either of the two interior positions xy or yz. By the union bound (only combinatorial counting; no probabilistic map independence),

    # occupied P5 >= max(0,
        [n-d_u-d_v](n-1)(n-2) -4m_X(n-2)).

For all genuine source W(3,s) alphabets, either n=3,m=0 (s=2), or n>=11 (s>=4). Thus n>=5 whenever m can be positive and 4(n-2)<=(n-1)(n-2). Because d_u+d_v+m_X<=m,

    # occupied P5 >= max(0,(n-m)(n-1)(n-2)).

This is true for EVERY physical typed injection and EVERY genuine original AA4-AL D pair. No source-index independence, no expectation and no artificial source incidence graph were used.

## 3. NEW AA4-AO2 — last near-triangular case m=a-3 still forces SOME occupied physical completion

If direct port-port physical edge IS occupied, use the residual K2+C3 family of AA4-AN: the edge {u,v} plus any triangle on three different physical auxiliary vertices of X is a valid four-edge residual. At most n edges have been removed globally from the original complete physical graph K_n on X. It therefore has at least C(n,2)-n > floor(n²/4) occupied edges for n>=7. By the **classical Mantel triangle-free extremal theorem** a graph on n vertices with more than floor(n²/4) edges contains a triangle. Thus at least one physically occupied K2+C3 residual exists. This proof is strictly combinatorial and does not use any original source-Hall acceptance.

Therefore if m<=a-3, **EVERY genuine actual AA4-AL original D pair has at least ONE physically occupied four-edge residual completion on EACH typed side** under ANY complete globally injected f,g.
